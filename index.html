About

📍 Redwood City, CA

🔗 CSCP — APICS

I'm a Principal Product Manager (Tech) with a career that bridges the physical world of manufacturing and supply chain with modern AI product practice. My edge is translating complex domain workflows — quality dispositions, BOM management, change orders, workforce qualification — into clean data models, scalable APIs, and AI products that operators and engineers actually adopt.

At Oracle, I own the roadmap for Manufacturing Cloud, shipping AI-native workspaces, autonomous agents, and digital-thread experiences to 15+ regulated factories across NA, EU, and APAC. Before that, at Cummins, I spent eight years transforming Lead-to-Cash processes — launching Salesforce CPQ, building a dealer portal that grew channel revenue from $20M to $90M, and applying Six Sigma to cut field defects by 70%.

I hold a Six Sigma Black Belt and CSCP, and I care deeply about responsible AI in regulated environments — guardrails, evals, and human-in-the-loop aren't afterthoughts in my practice, they're core design decisions.

Discovery:

  • Conducted on-site fieldwork at multiple dealerships — interviewed account managers, service technicians, and parts teams
  • Uncovered root causes beyond the assumed "parts lead time" hypothesis: catalog quality, technician readiness, warranty policy bottlenecks, and post-receipt scheduling gaps
