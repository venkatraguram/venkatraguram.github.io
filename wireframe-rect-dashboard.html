Top Insights & Recommended Actions
Synthesized from sample data · Prioritized by RECT impact
📚
Parts Catalog Quality — #1 Root Cause
34% of OOS events traced to incorrect part numbers in OEM catalogs. Mis-orders add avg 7.8 days per occurrence.
→ OEMs audit catalogs quarterly. Add part number validation at order entry.
−7.8d
⚖️
Warranty Authorization Bottleneck
Warranty repairs average 6.1 days waiting — vs 1.8 days regular. Range 2–18 days across OEMs. No standard SLA.
→ Establish 48-hr OEM warranty SLA. Pre-authorize common repair categories.
−4.3d
🔧
Technician Training Gaps
Uncertified tech repairs take 1.8× longer with 2.4× higher re-work. New model launches spike further.
→ Mandate OEM training prior to new model launch. Fund dealer certification.
−3.7d
📅
Seasonal Capacity Planning Failure
RECT spikes 72% in Q2–Q3. Dealers do not pre-position parts or staff for predictable seasonal demand.
→ Develop seasonal stocking guides. Incentivize pre-season certification.
−3.1d
🚐
Model Complexity Variance
Class A motorhomes average 2.3d longer RECT than travel trailers. Dealers with high Class A mix but low certification rates show worst outcomes.
→ Route complex repairs to certified dealers. OEMs publish complexity-tiered guides.
−2.3d
